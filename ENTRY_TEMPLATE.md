# Notebook entry template

Copy `entries/<date>-<slug>/index.html` from the newest entry and keep this order:

1. **Header:** title (plain words, no hype), date, two or three tags.
2. **Lead visual:** live video when one exists, otherwise the strongest figure. Caption says what the viewer is seeing and what the host versus the chip did.
3. **Claim:** one or two sentences, the result in plain words, bounded to what was tested.
4. **Numbers:** a small table, each value checked against its source, with exact counts preferred where rounded summaries obscure them, with its complement (n, the baseline, the control).
5. **The part worth knowing:** optional; the surprise or the trap a reader should take away.
6. **Limits box:** host versus chip roles, what was not tested, stronger and weaker controls and relevant failed checks, no speed or energy claim unless measured.
7. **Check it yourself:** available repository or release links (exact files), with missing or unrechecked artifact limits stated, the X post, data credits.

Rules: no em dashes; no "first" or novelty claims; AKD1500 reverse-engineering details (native rule, program/config formats, routing, selector state) never appear; wording checked by Astra before publishing; add the card to `index.html` (newest first) and to the profile README's latest list.
