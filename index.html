How we report

  • Evidence is identified. Entries link available code, result records or releases and state when an underlying artifact is not public or was not rechecked. Draft claims receive an additional internal, AI-assisted evidence check; this is not external replication or formal peer review.
  • Limits sit next to results. We report relevant failed checks and stronger as well as weaker controls. A chip result, a software reference, a replay and a fresh test are different claims.
  • Measured is kept apart from interpreted. When we say why something happened without having tested it, we say so.
  • Corrections are welcome. Open an issue on the linked repository or reply on X.
Newest first. Entries index available evidence; availability and verification limits are stated per entry.
